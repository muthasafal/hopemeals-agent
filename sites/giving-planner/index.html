Ask about your plan

The assistant can set your plan, and ask HopeMeals (student meals in Pune) what an amount funds and how its 80G receipts work. It shows you a card before it contacts HopeMeals, and HopeMeals' own window opens if you choose to donate.

Your messages go to the AI provider you pick in AI model settings, with your own key. Private details such as your PAN, phone or email are taken out on this device first; the card shows exactly what is sent.

How it works

  1. You plan here. Your total and months stay in this page.
  2. The assistant asks the charity. After you allow it on a card, it runs HopeMeals' published agent network in your browser, on your own AI key. HopeMeals answers only from its own site.
  3. You give on the charity's site. Donating opens HopeMeals' own page. This planner never takes money.

Pune Giving Planner is a demo of the Open Agentic Web, with airlock-gate keeping private details on your device. It is not a charity and holds no money.
