How it works

  1. You plan here. Your total and months stay in this page.
  2. The assistant asks the charity. After you allow it on a card, it runs HopeMeals' published agent network in your browser, on your own AI key. HopeMeals answers only from its own site.
  3. You give on the charity's site. Donating opens HopeMeals' own page. This planner never takes money.

Questions

Does the planner take or hold my money?

No. It only spreads a total across months. You give on the charity's own website.

Where do the figures about a charity come from?

From the charity itself. The assistant asks the charity's own published assistant, which answers only from its own site.

What happens to my PAN, phone or email if I type them?

They are removed on your device before anything is sent, and a card shows you exactly what goes out.

Who pays for the AI?

You use your own key with an AI provider you choose. Neither this site nor the charity sees the key.

Pune Giving Planner is a free demo of the Open Agentic Web, with airlock-gate keeping private details on your device. It is not a charity and holds no money.
