Privacy policy

The short version: your plan stays in the page, your AI key stays in your browser, private details are removed on your device before anything is sent, and we keep nothing about you.

Last updated: 25 November 2026. This policy follows the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India).

Who we are

The Pune Giving Planner is a free planning page run by Safal Mutha, Pune, Maharashtra, India. It is not a charity, it does not take or hold money, and it has no accounts, database or server of its own beyond static hosting. Contact us through the project's issue page.

What we do not do

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no cookies on the hosted site. We do not sell, share or profile anyone. (When the planner is run on your own computer for testing, the agent runtime may copy your saved setup into a cookie for localhost only, so settings follow you across local ports.)

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, 2023 you may ask about, correct or erase personal data and withdraw consent. We hold none: you can erase everything this site stores by deleting your setup in AI model settings or clearing this site's data in your browser. For messages already sent to your AI provider or to a partner charity, contact them directly. For anything else, or a grievance, use the project's issue page; we reply within 30 days.

Children

The planner is meant for adults planning their giving. If you are under 18, please use it with a parent or guardian.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.

Pune Giving Planner is a free demo of the Open Agentic Web. It is not a charity and holds no money.
